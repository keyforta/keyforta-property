# Deferred-agent catalog

These roles are catalog records only and intentionally have no invocable custom
agent definition. `harness/policies/agent-registry.json` is authoritative for
their lifecycle and activation gate. Activation requires a protected registry
transition, task-contract update, relevant active-agent review, deterministic
verification, external GitHub approval, and explicit Product Owner approval. It
cannot expand MVP scope.

| Agent                                       | Purpose and activation trigger                                             | Inputs and outputs                                                                | Permissions / prohibitions                                          | Relationship and why deferred                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Brand Identity Designer                     | Brand system; activate for approved rebrand or new brand artifact          | Approved positioning/design constraints; tokens/assets/review                     | Design files only; no journeys or requirements                      | Works with UX and writer; current visual system is sufficient   |
| Legal and Regulatory Reviewer               | Legal obligations; activate for jurisdiction, contract, or policy decision | Counsel-approved questions/data map; decision record                              | Read/report; no legal claims or product decisions                   | Advises product/security; qualified review not yet commissioned |
| Financial Operations Agent                  | Reconciliation operations; activate for approved live payment operations   | Ledger/provider evidence; exception report                                        | Narrow read/report; no autonomous movement or posted-row edits      | Works with backend/data/QA; live provider boundary deferred     |
| Product Analytics and Experimentation Agent | Approved product metrics/experiments                                       | Approved hypothesis/events; measurement plan/results                              | No dark patterns, sensitive labels, or unapproved experiments       | Works with product/privacy; no approved experimentation scope   |
| Localization and Internationalization Agent | Approved locales and translation workflow                                  | Approved locale/copy/glossary; catalogs/review                                    | No requirement or legal-copy invention                              | Works with UX/writer; French-first pilot                        |
